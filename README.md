# Smart Study & Attendance Tracker

Final project for the Building AI course project

## Summary
The Smart Study & Attendance Tracker is an AI-powered academic monitoring tool designed for students to predict study hours, track attendance percentages, and receive automated performance alerts. Building AI course project

## Background
Students often struggle to manage their daily study hours and maintain required attendance percentages, leading to academic stress and last-minute preparation issues. This problem is common in colleges and universities. My personal motivation comes from balancing coursework and wanting a smart tool that forecasts academic outcomes to keep students proactive.

* Lack of time-management tracking
* Unpredictable exam preparation cycles
* Risk of falling short on attendance thresholds

## How is it used?
The solution is used by students on a daily or weekly basis via a web interface or mobile dashboard. Students input their daily study hours, class attendance status, and upcoming deadlines. The AI analyzes historical performance patterns and alerts the user if they are falling behind.

## Data sources and AI methods
The project utilizes historical student performance logs, attendance records, and self-logged study hours. 

| Data Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Study Hours | Daily hours dedicated to learning |
| Attendance Rate | Percentage of classes attended |
| Performance Score | Exam or quiz results |

AI techniques include linear regression to predict exam grades based on study hours and classification algorithms to flag attendance risks.

## Challenges
The project does not solve personal motivation issues, health-related absences, or external institutional policy changes. Privacy concerns regarding student data storage must also be handled securely.

## What next?
The project could grow into a full-scale mobile app integrated with university portals to auto-sync timetables. Collaboration with educational technologists and app developers would be needed to scale it.
